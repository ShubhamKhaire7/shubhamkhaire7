## About

I work in **Business Strategy** at **thyssenkrupp Automotive Body Solutions** in Pune, driving company-wide AI and digitalization across Engineering, Procurement, Finance, HR and Commissioning.

I look at a business end to end: how work flows between departments, where decisions are made, and where time, cost and quality are lost. From there I identify where AI can be integrated to create measurable value, shape the business case, and then design, build and deploy the solutions myself, from agentic workflows and RAG assistants to data platforms and AI-enabled enterprise applications.
